Here's exactly what happens when a customer asks for a quote, a service, or an appointment — and why none of it ever leaves your systems.

A simple walkthrough of what happens the moment someone reaches out about a service, a pending quote, or scheduling — right up to the reply they get back.

The journey of a request

From a typed message to a finished quote or analytics report

This is the actual path a request takes through the platform — whether it comes in through the chatbot, a call with the Voice Agent, or a report requested from Better Genie.

1

A customer or franchisor initiates an interaction

End-customers reach out directly by typing into the chat widget or speaking with the AI Voice Agent (e.g., asking "I need my driveway pressure washed"). Meanwhile, franchisors ask Better Genie for performance insights and reports.

AI Voice Agent

2

Your own application picks it up

The message is received by the application we built for you, running privately inside your own AWS account — not on a shared server, and not visible from the public internet.

3

It checks who's asking and what your business actually offers

Before answering anything, the system confirms the customer's identity and pulls their record — and any pending quotes or past service history — from your own CRM. At the same time, it loads the exact services, questions, and pricing rules configured for that service, scoped to your specific business.

4

The AI decides what to say or ask next

That business context, together with the conversation so far, is sent once to AWS Bedrock (running Anthropic's Claude) — reachable only from inside your own environment, never over the public internet. The model doesn't invent pricing or policy; it simply decides, from what you've configured: ask the next question, confirm the service, or flag it for a person to review.

5

Live pricing, tax, and appointment slots come from your own systems

Once the service is confirmed, the actual quote total, tax, and product pricing come from your own CRM and pricing configuration — never from the AI. If the customer is booking, the system checks real technician and truck availability from your calendar, factoring in job duration and travel distance, and offers back only slots that are genuinely available.

Your CRM pricing & tax rules

6

Better Genie generates analytics securely via Amazon QuickSight

When asking Better Genie for business reports, customer data is aggregated and visualized using Amazon QuickSight inside your private environment. Datasets are protected with Row-Level Security (RLS) and KMS encryption, ensuring raw customer data is never exposed or transferred outside.

7

The customer gets their answer

The reply — a follow-up question, a finished quote, a confirmed appointment slot, or a generated report — is sent straight back through the same window the customer started in. Nothing is left behind with the AI.

Where all of this happens

Inside one place: your own secure environment

Every step above — the chatbot service, the CRM lookup, the AI, and QuickSight report generation — runs inside your company's own private AWS account. Nothing crosses out to the public internet, a shared system, or a third party at any point.

Your private AWS environment

No public doors

The AI is reached through a private connection inside your own environment. It has no public address and cannot be called from the open internet.

2

Only your application can use it

No outside vendor, franchise, or system has any way to call the AI directly. Only the application we built specifically for you can.

3

Keys locked in a vault

4

Your data stays yours alone

Each business's services, pricing rules, and customer records are kept separate in your own dedicated space.

  • Every piece of data processed by the AI remains 100% private, fully encrypted, and strictly isolated from the public internet
  • Nothing they say to the chatbot is stored by the AI itself

  • Every quote, price, and appointment comes from your own systems, not the AI
  • No outside company or vendor can ever see it
FAQs

Common questions

"Does it remember what I told it?"

Within a conversation, yes — so it doesn't ask you the same question twice, whether that's in the chatbot or on a call with the Voice Agent. But the AI model itself keeps no copy of what was said once the reply is sent; every answer is worked out fresh from your conversation and business records.

"Could my information end up training some AI somewhere?"

No. Your data is never used by AWS or Anthropic (the AI maker) to train, retrain, or improve any model. It's used once, to work out the reply, and that's it.
